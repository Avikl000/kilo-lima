Principles

Flat — no shadows, ever
Depth comes from layering, opacity and photography, not box‑shadow.
Hairline borders, not boxes
A 0.5px Runway hairline keeps white cards legible on white.
Photography does the work
Full‑bleed cinematic imagery; the UI frames it, never competes.
Motion is functional
200ms, standard easing. No bounce, parallax or scroll‑fades.
