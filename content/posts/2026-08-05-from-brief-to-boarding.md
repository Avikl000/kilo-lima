A charter aircraft is the easy part. What makes a charter work is the desk behind it — the people who take your brief, match it to the right aircraft and crew, and stay across every detail until you are on the ground at the other end.

## One call, one team

When you contact us you reach a person, not a form. That person owns your brief from the first call to the final leg. There is no queue to re-explain yourself to, and no handover you have to manage.

## We respond fast

During operating hours we come back to you within one business hour, and the desk is staffed around the clock for time-critical work. When a plan changes at short notice — and in aviation it does — you are talking to someone who can act.

## Have this ready

The more we know up front, the faster we can quote and confirm:

- Your dates, and how firm they are
- The number of passengers, and any freight or special equipment
- Where you need to depart from and arrive at, including any remote strips
- Anything that matters on board, from catering to accessibility

## We match the aircraft to the brief

We are not tied to a single airframe. We match the aircraft to the job — the King Air for regional and remote legs, larger aircraft for bigger groups or longer distances — and we are candid when a scheduled service or a different approach would serve you better.

From our base in Hamilton, Brisbane, we run hundreds of charters a year across Australia and the Asia-Pacific. The aircraft change. The standard does not.
