Most freight can wait for the next service. Some cannot. When a part, a sample or a shipment has to be somewhere today, a dedicated aircraft is not a luxury — it is the difference between a delay and a shutdown.

## When a charter earns its keep

We move the freight that scheduled services cannot handle on time:

- Aircraft-on-ground parts, where every hour a machine sits idle has a cost
- Pathology and medical consignments that are time and temperature sensitive
- Perishables and high-value goods that need to arrive fresh and intact
- Resupply to remote sites and communities when the usual run is cut

## Direct, and on your schedule

A charter flies point to point, on your timing, with no consolidation and no waiting for a connection. For a remote strip with no scheduled freight at all, it is often the only option — and we have the aircraft and the strips-experience to make it routine.

## Handled correctly

Time-critical does not mean corner-cutting. Dangerous goods are handled to the rules, temperature-sensitive loads are managed properly, and the desk coordinates the pickup and delivery at both ends so the aircraft is only ever part of a door-to-door plan.
