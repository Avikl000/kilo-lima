Owning an aircraft is straightforward. Operating one is not. The flying is the small part; behind it sit maintenance schedules, crewing, currency, insurance, hangarage and a wall of regulatory obligation. Aircraft management hands all of that to a team that does it every day.

## We run it, you fly it

We take on the operational load so you do not have to build a flight department of your own:

- Maintenance planning and oversight, with the right engineers and parts
- Crewing, training and currency, so a qualified crew is always available
- Regulatory compliance, insurance and hangarage, managed and documented
- Transparent reporting, so you always know the state and the cost of your asset

## Put the aircraft to work

An aircraft that sits on the ground still costs money. With your agreement, we can place your aircraft into charter when you are not using it, so it earns revenue that offsets the cost of ownership — on your terms, and around your own flying first.

## Two decades of doing exactly this

Managing aircraft to a professional operating standard is not a sideline for us; it is part of what we have done for close to twenty years. You keep the aircraft and the control. We carry the operation.
