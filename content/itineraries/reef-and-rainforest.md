Far north Queensland is the only place on earth where two World Heritage areas sit side by side — the Great Barrier Reef offshore, and the Daintree rainforest on the coast. Seeing both properly means covering distance, and the roads north of the Daintree are slow and often cut in the wet.

Flying opens the whole coast to you, all the way to Lizard Island on the northern reef. Small groups, private vessels and quiet beaches — the reef as it should be, away from the day-trip crowds.
