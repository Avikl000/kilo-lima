The Kimberley is the size of Germany, with a few thousand people and almost no sealed roads. That is exactly why it is best seen from the air. Our King Air lands on the short and unsealed strips that others cannot use, so a week covers ground that would take a month by road — and leaves the crowds behind.

Numbers are kept small. Eight guests, one aircraft, one tour manager. You travel on the country's own time, low and slow between the gorges, and stay in places most travellers never reach.
