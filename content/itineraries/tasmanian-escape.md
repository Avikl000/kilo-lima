Tasmania packs an extraordinary range into a small island — alpine country, rainforest, cool-climate wine and one of the best food scenes in the country. The catch is the roads, which are slow and winding, and the weather, which changes fast.

Flying the loop solves both. You cover the east and west coasts in a week without a single long transfer, and you keep the group to eight so the walks stay quiet and the tables stay small.
