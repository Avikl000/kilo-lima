The Red Centre rewards those who slow down, but its icons are hundreds of kilometres apart. Flying between them turns long, hot transfers into twenty-minute hops, so your time is spent walking the country rather than watching it pass a window.

We keep the group to eight and travel in the shoulder seasons, when the days are clear and the walking is comfortable. Every walk is guided, and wherever we can, it is led by the people whose country this is.
