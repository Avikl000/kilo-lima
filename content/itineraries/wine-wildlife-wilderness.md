South Australia is the quiet achiever of Australian travel — world-class wine, extraordinary wildlife and some of the oldest landscapes on the continent, all within a short flight of one another. The distances look modest on a map, but the drives are long and the best of it is spread wide.

Flying draws it together into a single unhurried week. From Barossa cellar doors to the sea lions of the Eyre Peninsula to the ramparts of the Flinders, you travel as a group of eight, at a pace that leaves room to linger.
